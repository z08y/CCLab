key characteristics:

  • generally calm and unbothered
  • minds its own business
  • silent
  • swallows the melancholy of those who approach it
  • willing to offer a hug

what we know so far about r-h-m:

according to the person who first discovered and observed it, r-h-m takes its name from a root in a certain human language that connects the words for uterus and compassion.
the creature appears to swallow the melancholy of those who approach it. the process seems to cause it bodily damage, yet it remains willing to offer an embrace.
these observations, however, remain interpretations. r-h-m has provided no account of its own behavior.
it has remained silent throughout every recorded encounter.
we do not know for certain what it is doing, only r-h-m itself does.


guidelines for human interaction with this creature

  • touch the creature's body
  • drag down into its body as if you're stabbing it. it'll begin to swallow your melancholy
  • watch as its body ruptures, turning your melancholy into amniotic-fluid like fragments
  • once it's fully swallowed your pain, the body will disappear, leaving its appendages to fold into an embrace
  • dont let go of the mouse if you want the hug to last longer
  • hugging too tightly can cause the creature to twitch
